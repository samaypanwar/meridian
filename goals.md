# Meridian goals — 2026-Q3 (Aug–Nov, front-loaded)

## Mission (6-month direction)

- Build strategic literacy in how real projects run on agent harnesses, by reading widely and running my own experiments.
- Publish in depth on samaypanwar.com, using writing to compress rigorous work for readers who will not follow the full derivation.
- Do data science in depth, with Kaggle as the lab that forces agents, modeling, and communication into one loop.
- Keep a steady reading practice across rationality, research craft, and RL that feeds better judgment over time.

## Themes

- `frontier/agentic-harnesses` — agentic coding tools, harnesses, orchestration patterns, model stacks, OpenRouter, OSS. What counts: anything that sharpens my picture of how projects run with agents today.
- `applied/data-science` — DS worked end to end; Kaggle as the integration lab. What counts: modeling, feature work, evaluation, and writeups that combine DS with agents and communication.
- `meta/communication` — writing that compresses hard technical work for a reader. What counts: blog drafts, writeups, TL;DRs, presentation craft.
- `meta/rationality-and-craft` — biases, decision-making, forecasting calibration, research craft (Hamming), and the operational learning loop. What counts: sources I route through spaced review to sharpen judgment.
- `foundations/rl` — Sutton & Barto and RL concepts, read for their own sake. What counts: chapters read and concepts captured. No implementation this cycle.
- `foundations/math` — linear algebra (QR, SVD, decompositions) and numerical issues. What counts: maintenance when a source needs it (e.g. sharding, numerics); not a coverage goal this cycle.
